[Pause 500 ms.]

Start with 6. Now take 3 and find where it belongs: before or after 6? Then do the same with each number that comes next.

[Pause 500 ms.]

Think of arranging cards in your hand. Take one card, make room for it, and put it where it belongs.

[Pause 500 ms.]
